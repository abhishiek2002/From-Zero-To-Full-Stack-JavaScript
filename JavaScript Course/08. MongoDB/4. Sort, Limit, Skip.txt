db.collectionName.find().count() 👉 Give us the number of documents in a collection we have.
db.collectionName.find().limit(2) 👉 Allows us to limit our query.(Give only specified numbers of documents or query)
db.collectionName.find({}, {"name": 1}).sort({"name": 1}) 👉 Apply Asending Order on name
db.collectionName.find({}, {"name": 1}).sort({"name": -1}) 👉 Apply Desending Order on name
db.collectionName.find({}, {"name": 1}).sort({"name": 1}).skip(1) 👉 Allows us to skip (amountProvided) query(documents)
